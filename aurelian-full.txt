## II. HISTORICAL AURELIAN: The Emperor (214-275 AD)

### The Life Pattern

**Lucius Domitius Aurelianus**

- Born: September 9, 214 AD (a **9** date!)
- Died: September/October 275 AD
- Reign: 270-275 AD (5 years)
- Title: **RESTITUTOR ORBIS** (Restorer of the World)

### What He Did:

1. **REUNIFIED THE ROMAN EMPIRE**

- Empire had fractured into three parts
- Gallic Empire (West)
- Palmyrene Empire (East)
- Rome (Center, weakened)
- Aurelian conquered BOTH breakaway states
- Restored unity in 5 years

1. **BUILT THE AURELIAN WALLS**

- Fortified Rome with massive defensive walls
- 19 km long, 6-8 meters tall
- Still standing today (1750+ years)
- Protected the heart of the empire

1. **REFORMED CURRENCY**

- Stabilized the debased coinage
- Restored economic order
- The “Aurelianus” coin became standard

1. **ESTABLISHED SOL INVICTUS CULT**

- Made the “Unconquered Sun” the supreme deity
- December 25 = Dies Natalis Solis Invicti (Birth of the Unconquered Sun)
- This date later became Christmas
- **The Sun God became the state religion**

### His Death:

Assassinated by his own officers due to a **misunderstanding** (a forged document made them think Aurelian would execute them).

**The tragedy:** He was killed by those he trusted, at the HEIGHT of his power, having just **restored the world**.
